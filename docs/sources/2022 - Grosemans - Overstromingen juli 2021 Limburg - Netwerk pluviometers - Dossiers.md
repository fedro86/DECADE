# Overstromingen juli 2021 Limburg - Netwerk pluviometers - Dossiers

- **Authors, year, venue:** written question no. 396 by Karolien Grosemans (Flemish Parliament), 20 January 2022, to Zuhal Demir, Flemish Minister of Environment; coordinated answer of the minister with input from VMM, RMI and the Hydrological Information Centre (HIC). The answer carries no date in the text read.
- **Link:** [docs.vlaamsparlement.be, pfile 1810499](https://docs.vlaamsparlement.be/files/pfile?id=1810499)
- **Access:** open (parliamentary document); no licence stated.
- **Read:** full text (6 pages).

## What it is

A parliamentary question on the July 2021 floods in Limburg: why RMI and VMM reached different conclusions for some municipalities, how many gauges exist, how gauges and radars are maintained. The coordinated answer is the only public text found that states which radars VMM uses, as opposed to the RMI.

## Numbers we use

All from the coordinated answer; read on 11/11/2026.

- **Radars used by VMM: Jabbeke and Helchteren**; radars used by the RMI: Jabbeke, Helchteren, Wideumont, Zaventem and Avesnois. The RMI images are "gecorrigeerd voor hagel, en atmosferische omstandigheden"; different radars and corrections make the two end products "licht verschillend" (answer 6a).
- VMM radar images are "afgeijkt aan grondpluviografen" and give the rain volume for every pixel of a quarter km² (answer 1).
- 52 VMM ground gauges in Flanders, 9 of them in Limburg (answer 1). The 2016 press release and the 2022 service documentation give 43 (see `2026 - RMI and VMM - Belgian radar network changes 2016–2026.md`).
- Maintenance of the VMM radar: twice a year by the manufacturer of the radar system; VMM gauges serviced about every 2 months, calibrated yearly by a certified contractor (answer 3).
- RMI radars Wideumont and Jabbeke: maintained by RMI technicians with support of the manufacturer (Leonardo), monitored permanently (answer 3).
- No new VMM gauge sites planned at the time (answer 5).
- Calamity thresholds: more than 35 mm/h or more than 70 mm/24 h (answer 6b).

## Relevance to DECADE

H3 (#20), VMM as national bridge across 7/2024: confirms that, at least in early 2022, the VMM product rested on two radars (Jabbeke, Helchteren) and was distinct from the RMI composite. It conflicts mildly with the waterinfo service documentation of 02/2022, which lists Herwijnen (KNMI) as a third radar, so the date at which Herwijnen entered the VMM composite is unknown. The twice-yearly manufacturer maintenance of Helchteren means small calibration steps are possible at any time; none is documented publicly.
