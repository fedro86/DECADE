## What it is

A short history and status of OPERA: from the pilot data hub to the ODYSSEY
data centre, the change of policy on input data, and the network in 2018. It is
the earlier, shorter version of Saltikoff et al. (2019).

## Numbers we use

- **Central QC from late 2015** (section 2, page 2): central clutter
  cancellation ran from late 2015; it was not good enough and "was destroying
  some real precipitation", which was later corrected. **No date is given for
  that correction.**
- **Satellite cloud mask** (section 2, page 2): from late 2015 the central
  processing removes more unwanted echoes with non-meteorological echo detection
  and a satellite-based cloud mask.
- **Input change** (section 2, page 2): in March 2017 a new agreement asked
  members to send their best possible data, to document the processing, and to
  send the unfiltered reflectivity (TH) as a separate parameter. **By May 2018,
  11 members had implemented it.** Belgium is not named.
- **Beam blocking and satellite removal of residual clutter** changed the amount
  of non-precipitating echoes from the end of 2015 (section 3, page 2); the
  monthly clutter fraction fell at the end of 2015 (page 3, from Haase and
  Johnson 2018).

## Relevance to DECADE

PR #96: dates the 3/2017 input change and shows that it was applied country by
country (11 members by 5/2018), so a step in a given region can come later than
3/2017. It also documents that the late-2015 QC removed real rain for a while,
which bears on our 12/2015 intensity step.

## Limits

Extended abstract, no technical detail; no per-country dates; the fix of the
"destroying real precipitation" problem is undated.
