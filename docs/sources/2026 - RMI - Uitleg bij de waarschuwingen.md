# Uitleg bij de waarschuwingen (RMI weather warning criteria)

- **Authors, year, venue:** Royal Meteorological Institute of Belgium (KMI /
  IRM), web page, undated, read on 10/11/2026
- **Link:** https://www.meteo.be/nl/weer/waarschuwingen/uitleg
- **Access:** free web page; no licence stated. No PDF saved.
- **Read:** the whole page (general principles, area definitions, criteria
  tables for rain and thunderstorms, damage note).

## What it is

The official criteria for the RMI colour-coded warnings (yellow, orange, red),
issued per Belgian province several times a day, following European
principles.

## Numbers we use

- **Rain and thunderstorm thresholds** (same values for both; l/m² = mm):
  - yellow: 20–30 mm in 1 h, or 20–40 mm in 6 h, or 25–50 mm in 24 h;
  - orange: 31–50 mm in 1 h, or 41–60 mm in 6 h, or 51–100 mm in 24 h;
  - red: above 50 mm in 1 h, or above 60 mm in 6 h, or above 100 mm in 24 h,
    or serious flooding already present with more heavy rain coming.
- **Thunderstorm extras:** hail 1–2 cm (yellow), 3–5 cm (orange), above 5 cm
  (red), plus gust criteria.
- Hourly amounts are forecast only 1 h ahead (footnote to the table).
- **Area words:** "very local" below a quarter of a province, "local" a quarter
  to a half, "widespread" at least half.
- **Provincial code** applies when the thresholds are exceeded with at least
  65% probability over at least a quarter of the province, or for the next
  higher code over a smaller area or with 15–65% probability.
- **Lead time:** yellow up to 48 h, orange up to 24 h, red at most 12 h ahead.
- The RMI notes that it forecasts the weather, not the damage, which also
  depends on local factors such as drainage.

## Relevance to DECADE (D3)

The only official Belgian numbers found for heavy and convective rain. The
1 h thresholds (20, 31 and above 50 mm) are natural D3 intensity classes for
accumulations, and the 1 / 6 / 24 h set matches the duration axis. Issued
warnings (orange or red for rain or thunderstorm) are a documented, external
proxy for "known impact" when choosing events, as in Franch et al. (2020).

## Limits

Forecast criteria for provinces, not an observed-event definition; no minimum
area in km². The page is undated and may change; keep the access date. Values
are close to the DWD warning level 3 used by CatRaRE (25 mm in 1 h).
