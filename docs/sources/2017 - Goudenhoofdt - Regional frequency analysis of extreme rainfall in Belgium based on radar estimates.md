# Regional frequency analysis of extreme rainfall in Belgium based on radar estimates

- **Authors, year, venue:** E. Goudenhoofdt, L. Delobbe, P. Willems, 2017,
  *Hydrology and Earth System Sciences* 21, 5385–5399
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-21-5385-2017
- **Access:** open, CC BY 3.0 (p. 5385)
- **Read:** sections 1, 2 (data), 3.1–3.2 (method, 1 h extremes), 4.2–4.3 and
  5 (conclusions); 3.3 and the tables skimmed.

## What it is

RMI extreme-value analysis of 1 h and 24 h rainfall from the Wideumont radar
QPE (1 km, 5 min, 2005–2016), compared with Belgian gauges (Uccle and others),
then a regional frequency analysis for every radar pixel in Belgium.

## Numbers we use

- **1 h accumulations** are taken as the duration of convective storms; 24 h
  as that of widespread rain (section 2.3, p. 5388).
- **Independence of extremes** (section 3.1, p. 5389): two 1 h peaks are
  dependent if less than 12 h apart (consistent with the Belgian storm
  statistics of Goudenhoofdt and Delobbe 2013); 3 days for 24 h peaks.
- **Hail cap:** 55 dBZ in the QPE, about 80 mm/h with the convective Z-R
  relation, about 100 mm/h with the standard one (section 2.2, p. 5388).
- **Largest 1 h radar extremes** (section 4.2): 28/7/2006, several supercells
  over Belgium; 22/8/2011, squall line; 26/7/2008, stationary convective cell.
  The ten highest 1 h extremes at the stations all occurred in summer
  (section 5.1).
- **Pixel filter:** pixels with more than 50 exceedances of 12 mm/h in 1 h
  were treated as non-meteorological echo (section 4.3, p. 5395).
- Areas with a 10-year 1 h return level above 30 mm lie mostly beyond 100 km
  from the radar and are attributed to hail contamination (section 4.3).

## Relevance to DECADE (D3)

Belgian basis for using 1 h accumulations as the convective duration in D3,
and for a 12 h separation between independent convective events. It names
three well-documented Belgian convective extremes, but all before 2024, so
outside the CIRRUS test period. Return levels per pixel could support a
return-period criterion (like CatRaRE T5) if the maps were available.

## Limits

Single radar, 2005–2016; return levels are given as maps and fitted
parameters, not as one national threshold.
