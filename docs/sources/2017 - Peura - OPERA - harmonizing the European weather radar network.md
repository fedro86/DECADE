## What it is

An overview of the OPERA 4 programme (2013–2018): quality monitoring, solar
monitoring, the composites, and the work on wind farms and radio interference.

## Numbers we use

- **December 2015** (section 2, page 1): beam-blocking correction, removal of
  non-meteorological echoes "based on data analysis", and removal of residual
  clutter with satellite data were implemented in ODYSSEY. This is the most
  precise month given for the satellite filter in any source found.
- **Effect** (section 2, page 1, Fig. 2): pixels dry less than 20 % of the time
  (residual clutter) fell clearly from 2015 to 2016; some remained in the north,
  attributed to the poorer geostationary satellite data at high latitudes.
- **Input policy** (section 2, page 1): at the **end of 2016** OPERA
  recommended that members send clean data and document their cleaning; at the
  time of writing (2017) its implementation was "still pending". Saltikoff et
  al. (2018, 2019) date the same agreement to March 2017.
- The data hub is said to run "since 2012" (page 1), while the other OPERA
  papers say 2011.

## Relevance to DECADE

PR #96: confirms 12/2015 for the satellite filter and the beam-blockage
correction, and shows that the input-policy change was decided around the turn
of 2016–2017 but not yet applied in 2017.

## Limits

Short conference paper; no description of the algorithms or settings, no
per-country dates.
