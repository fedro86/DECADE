# Spatial and temporal evaluation of radar rainfall nowcasting techniques on 1,533 events

- **Authors, year, venue:** R. O. Imhoff, C. C. Brauer, A. Overeem,
  A. H. Weerts, R. Uijlenhoet, 2020, *Water Resources Research* 56,
  e2019WR026723
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2019WR026723 · KNMI record:
  https://www.knmi.nl/research/publications/spatial-and-temporal-evaluation-of-radar-rainfall-nowcasting-techniques-on-1-533-events
- **Access:** open access (to confirm)
- **Read:** abstract and records only

## What it is

Verification of several radar nowcasting methods (including pysteps) over the
Netherlands on 1,533 events selected systematically for 4 durations and 12
lowland catchments (6.5–957 km²).

## Numbers we use

- Events are chosen by **duration and catchment**, not by a
  convective/stratiform label; the convective/stratiform contrast appears as
  **season**: summer errors are about three times the winter ones.
- Skill is about twice as long downwind as upwind of the domain edge.

## Relevance to DECADE

Reference for event selection in nowcasting verification next to Belgium:
supports season as a coarse split in H1 and WP4, and catchment-based selection
for V5.
