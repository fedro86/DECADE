# Statistical characteristics of convective storms in Belgium derived from volumetric weather radar observations

- **Authors, year, venue:** E. Goudenhoofdt, L. Delobbe (RMI), 2013,
  *Journal of Applied Meteorology and Climatology* 52, 918–934
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1175/JAMC-D-12-079.1 · RMI record:
  https://orfeo.belnet.be/handle/internal/8922
- **Access:** paywalled
- **Read:** abstract and record only. Earlier work by the same authors from
  conference papers ([ECSS 2009](https://www.essl.org/ECSS/2009/preprints/P07-13-goudenhoofdt.pdf),
  [ERAD 2010 record](https://orfeo.belnet.be/handle/internal/8884?show=full)).

## What it is

Ten years of 5 min volumetric reflectivity from the RMI C-band radar at
Wideumont, clutter-filtered and processed with the TITAN storm tracker: more
than one million storms identified. Climatology of storm frequency, size,
duration and motion over Belgium.

## Numbers we use

- **40 dBZ:** TITAN reflectivity threshold that defines a storm (abstract).
- **35–36 dBZ and 10 km³:** threshold and minimum volume in the earlier
  conference work; 45 dBZ inside a storm to separate distinct cells.
- A storm is present somewhere in the domain 15% of the time, 35% in July and
  August; simple tracks last about 25 min, complex ones about 70 min; median
  motion 30 km/h towards the north-east.

## Relevance to DECADE

The Belgian reference for what "convective" means at RMI: supports 40 dBZ as
the convective threshold in H1 and D3, and gives frequencies against which to
check a classification (for example the share of convective times per month).

## Limits

Single radar and volumetric data (3D cells), not a 2D composite: the volume
threshold has no direct equivalent on OPERA `DBZH`. Period before 2013.
