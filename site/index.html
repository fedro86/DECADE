Project plan

DECADE

ODYSSEY 2 km / 15 min · 2012–2024→1 km / 5 min→IRENE pre-training

DEep-learning nowCasting from A harmonised DEcade of radar data. The project develops a method to harmonise the legacy OPERA archive to the resolution of the current products, built only on pan-European data so that it can later be applied to the whole OPERA domain, and tests it on a Belgian pilot domain: it produces and releases the harmonised archive for Belgium + 350 km and measures how much pre-training on that decade improves IRENE nowcasts. ODYSSEY is the European OPERA radar reflectivity composite of 2012–2024, at 2 km and 15 min; CIRRUS is the current one, since July 2024, at 1 km and 5 min. Rain rate is derived from reflectivity with Marshall-Palmer. IRENE is the neural-network nowcasting model of FBK, currently trained on recent data only. HydroScan with the University of Basilicata.

Data and quantities

Which data are used where, and when

The OPERA products we harmonise are reflectivity. Rain rate is derived with Marshall-Palmer; accumulations are obtained by summing the rate over time. The external references measure different quantities, so each comparison is made in their quantity and at their resolution: rain rate at 5 min with VMM, accumulations with RADCLIM, EURADCLIM, ERA5-Land and CERRA-Land. Details, periods and licences are in docs/data.md.

Risks

What can go wrong and how to respond

In order of severity: the first five touch the critical path.

G1 slips

Archive v1 comes out with interpolation and cleaning only; the level correction moves to v1.1 (H8). Phase 1 still starts before the holidays.

Call lost

Phase 1 on the 4090 with 1 seed and runs reduced to about 40% of the planned size, with the same compute for A, B and C: with larger runs it would not finish by January. In parallel P1, the request for a smaller HPC allocation, which also helps later requests. P1 adds work in the weeks just before G1 and G2: if the chosen call closes before G2, G1 is allowed to slip and the correction moves to v1.1.

Full archive too slow on CPU

If the cost measured in D1 does not fit in the two weeks of D6, the windows for phase 1 are produced first and the full archive follows in January. The February release (R1) does not change.

Long queues on Leonardo

With fewer than 2 nodes in parallel, phase 1 runs past January, and every wait in the queue between one 24-hour job and the next lengthens the run. Ask for concurrent jobs, consider the 4-day lprod queue and launch everything before 24/12.

IRENE not ready in time

T0 starts at the beginning of the project, with fake data, so that the data loader (T1) has a target before the dataset exists; T7 brings training to 4 GPUs before the small run (T3).

VMM: only six months through the API

The API serves only about the last six months: the ODYSSEY period needs the data already downloaded by the project, scattered in 2021–2022 and almost continuous from 2023. If they are not enough, validation relies on RADCLIM, which reaches November 2023. VMM data do not enter the released archive.

No adequate pan-European bridge

If ERA5-Land and CERRA-Land are too coarse (EURADCLIM ends in 2023), a national bridge is used for Belgium: VMM, or Météo-France or Met Office from the MLCast catalogue (not RADCLIM, which ends in 2023). The bridge for Europe then becomes a stated limitation and future work.

End of the intensive phase in February

Every result of Marìca's goes into opera_ds/ by February: no method that lives only in a notebook. The 3-month extension is possible, but the plan does not take it for granted.

The code does not run on Windows

Marìca and the workstation are on Windows. S0 finds this out in a few days and with a few GB, before the H tickets; Federico fixes it, and CI tests on Linux and Windows keep it from coming back.

Laptop too slow or full

The full crops are about 95 GB and sample over 12 years takes about 10 h: full downloads and long runs go on the workstation.
